The archived `ryoseikoku_1280.png` is an unmodified copy of the 1280-pixel rendition generated by Wikimedia Commons.

The planned province-boundary dataset will trace borders from this image, apply regional alignment to the project's authoritative Japan land polygon, and clip the result to that polygon. The distributed adapted material must also identify those changes, its version, and the applicable license.
