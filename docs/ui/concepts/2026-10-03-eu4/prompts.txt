## 案1

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished flat 16:9 raster screenshot concept for the Japanese Sengoku grand strategy PC game My Worlds on Freedom. This is a proposal mockup, NOT final game assets. Visual language: restrained black lacquer and dark indigo panels, thin antique gold borders and small Japanese ornamental corners, cream Japanese text, subtle texture, consistent styling across panels. No European baroque ornament. Most of screen shows a believable geographical relief map of central Japan, land muted olive green, sea blue teal, thin district boundaries and subtle faction color fills, with a few small existing-style family crest circles. Do not invent castle icons or castle/place names on map. No portraits of people. Top-left family identification uses a small existing-style white Oda family crest on black with text 織田家（信秀系） and 当主 織田信秀. Values and task content are illustrative. All visible Japanese labels must be crisp and accurate. Do NOT put duplicate prestige values elsewhere. Date is plain text, editable controls show gold bordered clickable surfaces. Render straight-on real UI, no perspective, no device frame. Include small footer caption 構想図・数値は仮. Large clean labels, clear visual hierarchy. One whole game screen per image, not a collage. Proposal 1: THIN HORIZONTAL HUD, maximum map visibility. Small heading at bottom left outside main game UI reads 案1｜薄型HUD. Upper strip height only 110px of a 1080px screen. Family identity at x20 to x240. Single horizontally arranged resource band at x255 to x1390. Ten resources distributed as 2 clear aligned rows with mini painted Japanese icons and TEXT LABELS: 金銭 12,480 / 人口 214,257; 統治 32 / 兵力 10,711; 軍事 28 / 兵糧 1,073; 外交 21 / 領有 5郡; 治安 50 / 威信 50. Below identity and resource strip a short narrow navy gold action row reads 評定 配下 技術 外交 and each is clickable. At top right an equally thin consistent box shows 1546年1月11日 in cream, next line status 停止中, and buttons 再生 and direct speed choices 1倍 2倍 4倍 8倍; 1倍 subtly highlighted. Tiny menu button メニュー at far right. Map uninterrupted below y145, no other panels. This shows compact persistent resources and quick access inspired by EU4. Focus on low height, readable metrics, about 82% map visibility.
