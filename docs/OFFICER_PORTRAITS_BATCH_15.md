# Officer portraits, batch 15

Ten new modern, realistic, chest-up officer icons were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, one call per officer. All are 1254 × 1254 transparent PNGs with no image text, hands, scenery or frame. Existing game artwork was used only as a **rendering-style reference**. No source images were copied into the game.

**Source limitation:** Only the two Imagawa officers marked "historical painted portrait" had a usable, person-specific historical image in this search. For the other eight, neither a verified portrait of the named person nor an exact-person *Nobunaga's Ambition* face could be located. Their images are **original interpretations**, not likenesses derived from a substitute person's face. The historic-context links below do not establish appearance. This distinction is important: a related officer's image must not be labeled as the requested officer's portrait.
