For each verified named image, its cropped game portrait was supplied directly to the generator as a specific visual reference. For each of the other four, prompts explicitly limited the supplied image to clothing/style and required a distinct face. The child and Yoshitaka portraits were regenerated after visual inspection to keep the tops of their court caps within the square canvas. All final images were visually inspected, are square `Format32bppArgb`, and have transparent upper-left pixels.
