Hojo Kagehiro (1548–1579) was a samurai of the Echigo Hojo clan, renowned for his fierce martial skill and strategic acumen on the Kantō front under Uesugi Kenshin. After succeeding his father in 1574, he earned the moniker "Oni Yagorō" for his battlefield prowess. During the Otate no Ran, he led forces in support of Uesugi Kagetora, directing assaults on key strongholds and serving as the chief commander within the besieged Otate.
